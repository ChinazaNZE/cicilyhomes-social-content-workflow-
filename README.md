# CicilyHomes Social Content Workflow

Complete documentation of the creative and content workflow for the CicilyHomes Estates Instagram carousel campaign.

## Project Overview

This repository documents the end-to-end process of creating a professional 3-slide Instagram carousel for **CicilyHomes Estates**.  

The campaign focuses on helping landlords in Abuja protect their properties by highlighting the risks of unvetted tenants and presenting verified tenants and verified listings as the solution.

### Objective
To create a clear, premium, and trustworthy carousel that:
- Addresses the problem of unvetted tenants
- Positions CicilyHomes as the reliable alternative
- Drives traffic to [cicilyhomes.com](https://cicilyhomes.com)

### Target Audience
Landlords and property owners in Abuja.

---

## Carousel Structure

### Slide 1 – The Problem
**Headline:** Still risking your Abuja property with unvetted tenants?

Focus: Highlight the risk and create urgency.

### Slide 2 – The Solution
**Headline:** Protect what you’ve built.  
Verified tenants only.

Focus: Present the solution with real proof (verified tenant notifications) and a premium bedroom visual.

### Slide 3 – Call to Action
**Headline:** Ready to protect your property?

Focus: Strong closing message with clear next step.  
**Primary CTA:** cicilyhomes.com

## Workflow Summary

1. Defined the core problem (unvetted tenants)
2. Developed clear and professional copy for each slide
3. Refined messaging for clarity and authenticity
4. Designed a clean, premium visual direction
5. Incorporated real product notifications as social proof
6. Generated and selected supporting visuals
7. Finalized caption and hashtags for Instagram, LinkedIn, and X
8. Documented the entire process
